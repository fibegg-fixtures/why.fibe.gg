# Production deployment

Your live site, shop, or app — running on the internet for real users, without becoming a sysadmin.

You've built a thing. A landing page for your bakery. A small online shop. A booking form. A portfolio. A little tool your team uses every day. You want it on the internet. You want it to *stay* on the internet. You don't want to learn what a load balancer is.

Fibe runs the thing for you. Quietly. Reliably. And it keeps doing that as your thing grows.

## What you actually get

- **A real address on the internet** — `your-bakery.com` or `your-shop.fibe.app`, whichever you prefer
- **Always on** — Fibe watches it, restarts it, keeps it healthy
- **HTTPS just works** — the green padlock from minute one, no certificate fiddling
- **Automatic backups** — you don't have to think about them
- **A quiet phone** — no 3am alerts, no "the server is down" emails

## When this is for you

- You have a landing page that absolutely cannot disappear during a campaign
- You run a small ecommerce shop and a missing afternoon means missing rent
- You built a tool for your team and everyone depends on it being there
- You don't have an IT person, and you don't want to be one

## And when you grow

Fibe stays with you. The same setup that runs your one-page site today runs the same way when you ship a full app to a thousand customers tomorrow. You upgrade the server, not the platform. You never need to "migrate to the real tools" — you started with them.

> Boring is exactly what running a real thing should feel like.
